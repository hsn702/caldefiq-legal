## Common questions

**How do I sign in?**
Use your email (with a password or a 6-digit code), **Sign in with Apple**, or **Continue with Google**. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use that same button again.

**I didn't get my 6-digit code.**
Check your spam or junk folder and wait a minute. Codes expire, so request a new one if needed.

**I forgot my password.**
On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot Password?** and follow the 6-digit code steps.

**How accurate are the AI photo scans?**
They are estimates. Portions, sauces and cooking oil are hard to see in a photo. Always check the result and edit it before you log it. You can also log food by search or barcode.

**Why can't I scan more today?**
The free plan includes 2 AI photo scans, 3 assistant messages and 3 barcode lookups a day. Premium includes 10 AI scans a day (up to 250 a month), 20 assistant messages a day (up to 350 a month) and unlimited barcodes. Failed scans don't count. Daily allowances reset at **midnight in your own time zone**; monthly ones at midnight on the 1st.

**Are my meal photos stored?**
No. Meal photos are sent securely to our AI provider (OpenAI) to be analysed and are not kept by CalDefiq. Only the result is saved. Progress photos you choose to save are kept in a private storage area only you can access.

**Does CalDefiq show ads?**
No. CalDefiq has no ads on any plan, ever.

**How do I turn reminders on or off?**
**Settings (gear icon on the Home tab) → Notifications.** Reminders are off until you turn them on, and you get at most 2 a day.

**Steps or walk distance look wrong.**
Steps come from your iPhone's motion sensor. Walk distance uses GPS, which can drift in cities or indoors. Check that CalDefiq has **Motion & Fitness** and **Location** permission in iPhone **Settings → CalDefiq**.

**How do I export my data?**
**Settings (gear icon on the Home tab) → Data & Privacy → Export my data.**

**How do I delete my account?**
**Settings (gear icon on the Home tab) → Data & Privacy → Delete account.** This permanently deletes your account and all your data, including progress photos. It can't be undone. If you have a Premium subscription, cancel it separately in your Apple ID settings (deleting your account does not cancel it).

## Premium and billing

**How do I cancel Premium?**
iPhone **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → CalDefiq → Cancel Subscription.** Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

**I bought Premium on a new phone / after reinstalling and it's not showing.**
Sign in to the same CalDefiq account, then tap **Restore Purchases** on the Premium screen.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: visit **reportaproblem.apple.com**.
