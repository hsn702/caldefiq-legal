# CalDefiq Support


---

## Need help with CalDefiq?

Email us at **[caldefiq@gmail.com](mailto:caldefiq@gmail.com)**. We aim to reply within 3–5 business days. You can also email us from the app: **Profile → Help & Support → Email support** (open Profile with the person or gear icon at the top of the Home tab).

To help us help you, please include:
- your iPhone model and iOS version;
- the CalDefiq version (shown at the bottom of the **Profile** screen);
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened (a screenshot helps);
- the email you sign in with (**never** send us your password or a verification code).

## Common questions

**How do I sign in?**
Use your email and password, **Sign in with Apple**, or **Continue with Google**. When you create an account with email, we send a 6-digit code to confirm your address. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use that same button again.

**I didn't get my 6-digit code.**
Check your spam or junk folder and wait a minute. Codes expire, so request a new one if needed.

**I forgot my password.**
On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot Password?** and follow the 6-digit code steps.

**How accurate are the AI photo scans?**
They are estimates. Portions, sauces and cooking oil are hard to see in a photo. Always check the result and edit it before you log it. You can also log food by search or barcode.

**Why can't I scan more today?**
The free plan includes 2 AI photo scans, 3 assistant messages and 3 barcode lookups a day. Premium includes 10 AI scans a day (up to 250 a month), 20 assistant messages a day (up to 350 a month) and unlimited barcodes. Failed scans don't count. Daily allowances reset at **midnight in your own time zone**; monthly ones at midnight on the 1st.

**Are my meal photos stored?**
Every scanned photo is sent securely to our AI provider (OpenAI) to be analysed. When you **log** a meal from a scan, CalDefiq saves its photo **privately** in your account so you can see it in your Diary and Scan History; only you can see it. To stop saving photos, turn off **Profile → Save meal photos**. To delete one photo, open the meal in your Diary and tap **Delete photo**; to delete them all, use **Profile → Data & Privacy → Delete all meal photos**. Progress photos are also kept in private storage only you can access.

**Why can't I see a walk's route map on my new phone?**
For your privacy, detailed walking routes (GPS) are kept only on the phone that recorded them. Your walk's time, distance, steps and calories are saved to your account and appear on every phone, but the route map doesn't move to a new phone and is lost if CalDefiq is deleted.

**What does Clear chat delete?**
The text of your conversation with CalDefiq AI, including any suggestions. CalDefiq keeps only a usage record without text (time and AI cost), so daily limits keep working.

**Does CalDefiq show ads?**
No. CalDefiq has no ads on any plan, ever.

**How do I turn reminders on or off?**
**Profile → Notifications.** Reminders are off until you turn them on, and you get at most 2 a day.

**Steps or walk distance look wrong.**
Steps come from your iPhone's motion sensor. Walk distance uses GPS, which can drift in cities or indoors. Check that CalDefiq has **Motion & Fitness** and **Location** permission in iPhone **Settings → CalDefiq**.

**How do I export my data?**
**Profile → Data & Privacy → Export my data.**

**How do I delete my account?**
**Profile → Data & Privacy → Delete account.** This permanently deletes your account and all your data, including progress photos. It can't be undone. If you have a Premium subscription, cancel it separately in your Apple ID settings (deleting your account does not cancel it).

## Premium and billing

**How do I cancel Premium?**
iPhone **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → CalDefiq → Cancel Subscription.** Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

**I bought Premium on a new phone / after reinstalling and it's not showing.**
Sign in to the same CalDefiq account, then tap **Restore Purchases** on the Premium screen.

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple: visit **reportaproblem.apple.com**.
