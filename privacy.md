# CalDefiq Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information the CalDefiq app collects, why, who processes it, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

**Who is responsible for your data:** CalDefiq is operated by [[OWNER: operator legal name — see note before publishing]] ("CalDefiq", "we", "us"), who decides how your data is used (the "data controller").
**Contact:** [caldefiq@gmail.com](mailto:caldefiq@gmail.com) for any privacy question or request.

---

## 1. Summary

- CalDefiq is a calorie, nutrition and fitness tracker. It stores what you log (meals, weight, workouts and similar) in your private account.
- **No ads, ever.** CalDefiq contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
- When you scan a meal photo, the photo is sent to **OpenAI** for analysis. **We do not keep the photo.**
- You can **export** your data or **delete your account** in the app at any time: **Profile → Data & Privacy** (open Profile with the person or gear icon at the top of the Home tab).

## 2. Information we collect and why

"Your account" means our database, hosted by Supabase (section 5).

### 2.1 Account information
| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address | To create your account, sign you in, and send email-verification and password-reset codes. |
| Password (email sign-up only) | To sign you in. It is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider (Supabase Auth). We cannot see it. |
| Name (optional) | To greet you in the app. With **Sign in with Apple**, Apple may share your name the first time you sign in; we keep only the first name. |
| Sign-in provider details | With **Sign in with Apple** or **Continue with Google**, that provider confirms your identity and shares your email address. Apple may give a private "Hide My Email" relay address instead. |
| User ID | A random code that links your data to your account. |

### 2.2 Body and health information you enter
Sex, age (stored as birth year), height, weight, activity level, goal (lose, maintain or gain), target weight, calorie and macro targets, weight history, optional body measurements (waist, hips, chest, arm, thigh, neck, body-fat percentage) and notes.
**Why:** to calculate your targets and show your progress. **This is health information.** We use it only to provide CalDefiq to you.

### 2.3 Food, water and fitness logs
Meals and foods you log (name, portion, calories, protein, carbs, fat), saved meals and recipes, custom foods, corrections, water intake, workouts (type, duration, sets, reps, weights, distances, estimated calories burned) and your step goal.
**Why:** this is the core of the app.

### 2.4 Steps and recorded walks
- **Steps:** with your permission ("Motion & Fitness"), CalDefiq reads your iPhone's daily step count and stores one total per day in your account, so it follows you to a new phone.
- **Recorded walks:** only after you tap **Start** on a walk, CalDefiq uses your location to draw your route and measure distance and calories. If you allow it, tracking continues while your phone is locked, until you tap **Finish**. We store the walk's start and end time, distance, steps, calorie estimate and **route (GPS points)**. Nothing is recorded before Start or after Finish, and you can delete any walk.

### 2.5 Country detection
If you tap the button to detect your country, CalDefiq asks for your location once, at the lowest accuracy, and converts it to a country on your phone using Apple's location service. **The coordinates are discarded**; only the country you confirm is saved. You can also choose your country manually.

### 2.6 Region and preferences
Country, region, language, time zone, currency, units, favourite cuisines, reminder settings and other app preferences.
**Why:** to show the right units, local dishes and prices, and to reset daily limits at midnight in your own time zone.

### 2.7 Photos
- **Meal photos for AI scanning:** when you scan a meal (or a "Before & After" leftovers photo), the photo is sent securely from your phone to our server, and from there to **OpenAI** for analysis. **We do not store the photo.** We store only the result (foods, portions and nutrition estimates) so you can see your scan history. Temporary copies on your phone are deleted after use.
- **Progress photos:** if you add progress photos, they are kept in a **private** storage area that only your account can access, and shown only through short-lived links. They are never public.
- **Saving images:** if you save an image (for example a share card) to Photos, CalDefiq asks only for permission to *add* to your library. It does not read your library.

### 2.8 AI scan records
For each AI scan we keep the date and time, status, the AI's result, the result you confirmed, and technical usage data (AI "tokens" used and estimated cost). If you delete a scan, its content is erased and hidden, but a bare record (time, status, tokens, cost) is kept so daily limits and cost controls cannot be bypassed. That bare record is deleted when you delete your account.

### 2.9 CalDefiq AI assistant
- **Messages:** we store your messages and the assistant's replies so you can see the conversation. Messages about sensitive topics (for example medical conditions, eating disorders or self-harm) are labelled so the assistant can respond carefully. You can clear your history in the app; cleared messages are emptied and hidden, and fully deleted when you delete your account.
- **Your records (optional, off by default):** only if you turn on **Use my CalDefiq records**, the assistant reads a summary of your last 7 days (meals, water, daily step totals, workouts, walks, weigh-ins and goals), read-only. The same applies to the optional weekly progress report. You can turn it off at any time.
- **How replies are written:** your message, and the 7-day summary only if you turned it on, are sent to **OpenAI** to write the reply. Some simple questions (such as "how many calories do I have left?") are answered by CalDefiq itself without AI. We never send your name, email or account ID. The assistant can suggest adding water or a food, but nothing is added unless you confirm.

### 2.10 Food budget and shopping
If you use the Budget Planner, we store your budget, the food spending you enter (amount, currency, category, note), ingredient prices, pantry items and shopping list.

### 2.11 Food search and barcodes
Search words and barcode numbers go to our server, which looks them up in **USDA FoodData Central** and **Open Food Facts**. We do **not** send your name, email or account ID to them. We count lookups per day (for daily limits) but do not keep a history of your searches.

### 2.12 Premium and usage records
- Daily (and, for Premium, monthly) counts of AI scans, barcode lookups and assistant messages, to apply plan limits.
- If you subscribe to Premium: plan (monthly or yearly), status, renewal and expiry dates, the App Store product and transaction IDs. **Payment is handled entirely by Apple.** We never see or store your card or bank details.

### 2.13 Reminders
Reminders are **local notifications** scheduled on your phone and are off by default. We store only your reminder settings. We do not send marketing notifications.

### 2.14 What we do NOT collect
- No advertising identifiers (IDFA), ad networks, ad tracking or data brokers.
- No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software.
- No contacts, microphone, browsing history or data from other apps.
- No payment card details.

## 3. Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is **optional**. If you connect it, CalDefiq **reads** (never writes) **steps, walking and running distance, workouts and active energy**. You can turn access off at any time in iPhone **Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → CalDefiq**.

As Apple requires, data from Apple Health:
- is used **only** to show your activity and calorie information in CalDefiq;
- is **never** used for advertising or marketing;
- is **never** sold, or shared with advertisers, data brokers or information resellers.

**What is saved to your account,** so your history is the same on every device:
- one **step total per day**: the higher of your iPhone's count and Apple Health's total (never added together);
- a summary of each **imported workout**: type, start and end time, duration, distance, active calories, and the name of the recording app or device (for example "Apple Watch").

Duplicate workouts are not counted twice, and a workout you delete in Apple Health stops counting. You can disconnect Apple Health and delete everything CalDefiq imported from it at any time (**Profile → Apple Health**). This does not change anything in Apple Health itself.

If you turn on **Use my CalDefiq records** for the assistant, your daily step totals (which may include Apple Health steps) are part of the 7-day summary (section 2.9). Imported workouts are not sent to the assistant.

## 4. Home Screen widgets

CalDefiq widgets show today's summary (calories left, protein, carbs, fat, steps and water). The app shares this summary with the widget **on your phone only** (through an iOS "App Group"). Anyone who can see your screen may see it. When you log out, the widget stops showing your data.

## 5. Service providers and third parties

We use these providers to run CalDefiq. They process data on our behalf, only to provide their service.

| Provider | What they do | What they receive |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Database, sign-in, private photo storage and server functions. Also sends verification and password-reset emails, directly or through its email delivery service. | The account data described above. Database region: **AWS ap-northeast-2 (Seoul, South Korea)**. |
| **OpenAI** | Analyses meal photos; writes assistant replies and weekly reports. | The photo (for Before & After, also the food names from your earlier scan); for the assistant, your message and, only if you turned it on, the 7-day summary. **Not** your name, email or account ID. We ask OpenAI not to store requests (`store: false`). Under OpenAI's API data policy, requests may still be kept **for up to 30 days** to detect abuse, then deleted, and OpenAI does not use API data to train its models by default. |
| **USDA FoodData Central** | Food nutrition database (US government). | Search words and food names, sent from our server. |
| **Open Food Facts** | Packaged-product database. | Barcode numbers, sent from our server. |
| **Apple** | Sign in with Apple, App Store payments, maps during walks, and on-device country detection. | What you share with Apple under Apple's privacy policy. |
| **Google** | Only if you choose "Continue with Google". | What you share with Google under Google's privacy policy. |

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

## 6. International transfers

Your data is stored in South Korea (Supabase) and processed in the United States (OpenAI, USDA) and other countries where our providers operate. These may be different from where you live and may have different data-protection laws. Where the law requires, we rely on our providers' contractual safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses in their data-processing terms) for these transfers.

## 7. Legal bases (EU, UK and similar laws)

Where these laws apply, we process:
- your account and logs **to provide the service you asked for** (contract);
- **health information** you enter or connect **with your explicit consent**, which you can withdraw at any time by deleting that data, disconnecting Apple Health or deleting your account;
- limited usage and cost records (for example scan counters) for our **legitimate interest** in preventing abuse and keeping the service affordable;
- records we must keep **to comply with the law**.

## 8. How long we keep data

- **While your account exists:** we keep your data so the app works.
- **When you delete an item:** meals, walks, photos and other items are deleted straight away. Deleted AI scans and cleared assistant messages keep only a bare record until you delete your account (sections 2.8 and 2.9).
- **When you delete your account:** your account and all data linked to it are deleted from our database, and your progress photos are deleted from storage.
- **Backups:** our hosting provider keeps automatic database backups for a limited time (up to 7 days), after which deleted data is overwritten.
- **Technical logs:** server logs kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting are deleted automatically (within 7 days).
- **OpenAI:** up to 30 days, as described in section 5.

## 9. Your rights and choices

- **Access and export:** Profile → Data & Privacy → **Export my data** (a copy in JSON format).
- **Correct:** edit your information in the app.
- **Delete:** delete single entries in the app, or everything with Profile → Data & Privacy → **Delete account**.
- **Withdraw consent:** turn off Apple Health, "Use my CalDefiq records", location, motion, camera, photos or notifications at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.
- **Other requests:** email [caldefiq@gmail.com](mailto:caldefiq@gmail.com). We may need to confirm the request comes from the account owner (for example, by replying from your account email).

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or other regions with privacy laws), you may have additional rights, such as objecting to or restricting processing, and the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. We do not sell or "share" personal information as defined by California law.

## 10. Children

CalDefiq is not intended for children. You must be at least **13 years old** to use it (or older if the law where you live requires it). Users under 18 cannot set a calorie-deficit goal. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Security

Database rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change your data. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Progress photos are stored privately and shown only through short-lived links. Secret keys are kept on our servers, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

## 12. Health disclaimer

CalDefiq provides estimates, not medical advice. AI and food-database values can be wrong. Talk to a doctor before making major changes to your diet or exercise, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, or have a history of eating disorders.

## 13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
