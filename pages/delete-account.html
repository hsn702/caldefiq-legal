1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open CalDefiq and go to Profile (the person or gear icon at the top of the Home tab).
  2. Tap Data & Privacy.
  3. Optional: tap Export my data first to keep a copy.
  4. Tap Delete account, read the confirmation, and tap Delete everything.

The deletion runs on our server straight away. When it finishes, you are signed out and the app returns to the welcome screen. This cannot be undone.

Cancel Premium separately. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings first. See {{link:subscriptions#cancel}}.

2. What is deleted

  • Your account: your sign-in, email address and profile.
  • Everything linked to it in our database: meals, foods, recipes, water, weight and measurements, workouts, walk summaries, steps, goals, settings, budgets, Apple Health imports, AI scan history and usage records, assistant conversations and usage records, Premium status records and complimentary access.
  • Your photos: every progress photo and saved meal photo in your private storage. The server removes the files first and checks that none remain before deleting the account.
  • On the phone you delete from: walking routes stored on that phone, walks and meal photos still waiting to upload, and your data in CalDefiq’s Home Screen widget.

4. Delete only some data

  • Single items: delete any meal, weigh-in, workout, walk, recipe or photo where you see it in the app.
  • All saved meal photos: Profile → Data & Privacy → Delete all meal photos. Your food entries and scan results stay.
  • Assistant conversation: Clear chat in CalDefiq AI erases the text of your messages and replies (a usage record without text is kept for limits).
  • Apple Health imports: Profile → Apple Health lets you disconnect and delete everything CalDefiq imported.
  • Stop saving meal photos: turn off Save meal photos in Profile.

5. Can’t open the app? Ask us by email

If you no longer have the app or can’t sign in, email {{email}} with the subject “Delete my CalDefiq account”.

  • Write from the email address you use for CalDefiq, so we can confirm the request is yours. If you can’t, tell us and we’ll explain how else to verify it.
  • Tell us whether you also want your support emails deleted.
  • We will confirm when the deletion is complete. We respond within the time your local law requires.

Never send us your password or a sign-in code.
