Feature status (November 1, 2026). This policy describes CalDefiq including three privacy updates that are being rolled out and are not yet live on our servers: saving logged meal photos privately in your account, Clear chat also erasing safety labels and suggested actions, and a server safeguard that refuses to store walking routes. Until they are live: scanned meal photos are analysed but not saved in your account (the Save meal photos and Delete photo options apply once saving is live); Clear chat erases the text of your messages and replies but keeps each cleared message’s safety label and any suggested action, which are removed when you delete your account. We will update this notice when the rollout is complete.

3.2 Body and health information you enter

Sex, age (stored as birth year), height, weight, activity level, goal (lose, maintain or gain), target weight, calorie and macro targets, weight history, optional body measurements (waist, hips, chest, arm, thigh, neck, body-fat percentage) and notes, and whether you told us you are pregnant, breastfeeding or have a health condition that affects what you eat.

Why: to calculate your targets, apply safety limits and show your progress. This is health information. We use it only to provide CalDefiq to you.

3.3 Food, water and fitness logs

Meals and foods you log (name, portion, calories, protein, carbs, fat), saved meals and recipes, custom foods and corrections, water intake, workouts (type, duration, sets, reps, weights, distances, estimated calories burned) and your step goal. Why: this is the core of the app.

3.4 Steps and recorded walks (GPS)

  • Steps: with your permission (“Motion & Fitness”), CalDefiq reads your iPhone’s daily step count and stores one total per day in your account, so it follows you to a new phone.
  • Recorded walks: only after you tap Start on a walk, CalDefiq uses your location to draw your route and measure distance and calories. If you allow it, tracking continues while your phone is locked, until you tap Finish. Nothing is recorded before Start or after Finish.
  • What is stored where: your account stores the walk’s summary (start and end time, distance, steps and calorie estimate). The detailed route (GPS points) stays only on the phone that recorded it: it is not uploaded to our servers, does not move to a new phone, and is lost if CalDefiq is removed from that phone.
  • Deleting a walk deletes its summary and its route. Deleting your account also deletes the routes stored on the phone you delete it from.

3.5 Country detection

If you tap the button to detect your country, CalDefiq asks for your location once, at the lowest accuracy, and converts it to a country on your phone using Apple’s location service. The coordinates are discarded; only the country you confirm is saved. You can also choose your country manually.

3.6 Region and preferences

Country, region, language, time zone, currency, units, favourite cuisines, reminder settings and other app preferences. Why: to show the right units, local dishes and prices, and to reset daily limits at midnight in your own time zone.

3.7 Photos

  • Meal photos for AI scanning: when you scan a meal (or a “Before & After” photo), the photo is sent securely from your phone to our server, and from there to OpenAI for analysis. We store the result (foods, portions and nutrition estimates) in your scan history.
  • Saved meal photos: when you log a meal from a normal scan, CalDefiq saves that photo (resized, plus a small thumbnail) in a private storage area of your account, so you can see it in your Diary, the meal’s details and Scan History. Only your account can access these photos; they are shown only through short-lived private links and are never public. Photos of meals you don’t log, and Before & After photos, are not saved.
  • Turning it off: Save meal photos is on by default. You can turn it off in Profile: photos are then still analysed by OpenAI but not saved. Photos already saved stay until you delete them.
  • Deleting saved meal photos: a photo is deleted when you delete it, when you delete the last diary item of that meal, when you delete the scan from Scan History, when you use Profile → Data & Privacy → Delete all meal photos, or when you delete your account. If an upload fails (for example offline), the photo waits on your phone for up to 7 days and is retried automatically.
  • Progress photos: kept in a private storage area that only your account can access, shown only through short-lived links, and never public.
  • Saving images to Photos: if you save an image (for example a share card), CalDefiq asks only for permission to add to your library. It does not read your library.

3.8 AI scan records

For each AI scan we keep the date and time, status, the AI’s result, the result you confirmed, and technical usage data (AI “tokens” used and estimated cost). If you delete a scan, its content and saved photo are erased and the scan is hidden, but a bare record (time, status, tokens, cost) is kept so daily limits and cost controls cannot be bypassed. That bare record is deleted when you delete your account.

3.9 CalDefiq AI assistant

  • Messages: we store your messages and the assistant’s replies so you can see the conversation. Messages about sensitive topics (for example medical conditions, eating disorders or self-harm) are labelled so the assistant can respond carefully.
  • Clear chat: erases the text of your messages and replies, their safety labels and any suggested actions. Only a usage record without any text is kept for each message (time, whether it was yours or a reply, the AI model, token count, estimated cost and whether the AI call failed), because daily limits and cost controls depend on it. Usage records older than 62 days are deleted the next time you clear the chat, and all of them when you delete your account.
  • Your records (optional, off by default): only if you turn on Use my CalDefiq records does the assistant read a summary of your last 7 days (meals, water, daily step totals, workouts, walks, weigh-ins and goals), read-only. The same applies to the optional weekly progress report.
  • How replies are written: your message, and the 7-day summary only if you turned it on, are sent to OpenAI to write the reply. Some simple questions (such as “how many calories do I have left?”) are answered by CalDefiq itself without AI. We never send your name, email or account ID. The assistant can suggest adding water or a food, but nothing is added unless you confirm.

See also the {{link:ai-disclaimer}}.

3.10 Food budget and shopping

3.11 Food search and barcodes

4. Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you connect it, CalDefiq reads (never writes) steps, walking and running distance, workouts and active energy. You can turn access off at any time in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → CalDefiq.

As Apple requires, data from Apple Health:

  • is used only to show your activity and calorie information in CalDefiq;
  • is never used for advertising or marketing;
  • is never sold, or shared with advertisers, data brokers or information resellers.

What is saved to your account, so your history is the same on every device: one step total per day (the higher of your iPhone’s count and Apple Health’s total, never added together), and a summary of each imported workout (type, start and end time, duration, distance, active calories, and the name of the recording app or device). Duplicate workouts are not counted twice, and a workout you delete in Apple Health stops counting.

You can disconnect Apple Health and delete everything CalDefiq imported from it at any time in Profile → Apple Health. This does not change anything in Apple Health itself. If you turn on Use my CalDefiq records for the assistant, your daily step totals (which may include Apple Health steps) are part of the 7-day summary; imported workouts are not sent to the assistant.

5. Home Screen widgets

CalDefiq widgets show today’s summary (calories left, protein, carbs, fat, steps and water). The app shares this summary with the widget on your phone only (through an iOS “App Group”). Anyone who can see your screen may see it. When you log out, the widget stops showing your data.

6. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: your diary, targets, progress, AI scans, assistant, walks and Premium features.
  • To apply fair-use limits, prevent abuse and keep AI costs under control.
  • To answer your support requests.
  • To keep CalDefiq secure, investigate problems and comply with the law.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not build marketing profiles.

Automated processing: CalDefiq calculates calorie targets and AI estimates automatically, and applies plan limits automatically. These produce suggestions and limits inside the app; they do not make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

7. Support emails and our private support inbox

Support email to {{email}} is received by Gmail (Google). To answer you, we use our private Command Center, an internal tool that only the CalDefiq owner can open, protected by two-step verification. It connects to the support mailbox through Google’s official Gmail API.

  • What our database stores about a support conversation: the sender and recipient addresses, the sender’s display name, the subject, Gmail’s short preview of the message, dates, whether it is read, whether it has attachments, and our own ticket information (status, priority and internal notes).
  • What it does not store: the full text of emails and their attachments stay in Gmail. They are fetched from Gmail only when we open the conversation, and are not saved in our database.
  • Linking to your account: if the email comes from the same verified email address as a CalDefiq account, the conversation is linked to that account automatically, so we can see your plan and account status while helping you. We can also link or unlink a conversation by hand (for example if you wrote from another address); each manual change is recorded with a reason.
  • Replies are sent from {{email}} through Gmail and are kept in that mailbox.
  • Retention: support emails are kept in the Gmail mailbox, and conversation details in our support inbox, for as long as we need them to answer you, follow up on your request and keep a record of our support history. We review them periodically and delete those we no longer need; we have not set a fixed period. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence at any time.

Please don’t send passwords, verification codes or medical records by email.

8. Who at CalDefiq can see your information

CalDefiq is run by one person. The owner can use the private Command Center to look after accounts. It can show account and plan information (email, name, country, time zone, language, currency, sign-up date, email verification, last activity, Premium or complimentary status, daily usage counts and estimated AI cost).

The Command Center does not show your meals, weights, measurements, photos, walking routes, health data or assistant conversations.

Administrative actions (for example giving complimentary access, changing a limit for an account, suspending an account, linking a support conversation, or sending a support email) require a typed reason and two-step verification. They are recorded in an append-only audit log that cannot be edited.

Regional rights

We respond to requests within the time your local law requires.

14. Children

CalDefiq is not intended for children. You must be at least 13 years old to use it, or older if the law where you live requires it. Users under 18 cannot set a weight-loss goal. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Security

  • Database access rules allow only your signed-in account to read or change your data.
  • Connections between the app, our servers and our providers use HTTPS encryption.
  • Progress photos and saved meal photos are stored privately and shown only through short-lived links.
  • Secret keys stay on our servers, never in the app.
  • The owner-only Command Center requires two-step verification, and its administrative actions are logged.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires it.
